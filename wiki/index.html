Prakhar Prasun (Sanskrit: प्रखर प्रसून; born July 30, 2004), better known mononymously by his nickname Ark (formerly designated Xaarkph during his brief canonical tenure as an Organization XIII Nobody, and xarkph as a rogue gamer tag), is an Indian undergraduate Mechanical Engineering student at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, an aspiring computational neuroscientist, and a competitive problem-solver.

Known for his deep interest in brains, biological connectomes, chess, video games, fiction writing, and K-pop, Ark combines the analytical habits of an INTP with sudden, high-intensity hyperfixations—most famously reading the entirety of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 7: Steel Ball Run in three days as a form of "efficient" procrastination. In K-pop idol profile terminology, his official representative colour is Black and his representative emoji is 😭.

Etymology and Nickname Lore 🗝️ Kingdom Hearts Canon

While casual acquaintances frequently assume that Ark is simply a three-letter syllable extracted from his birth name (Prakhar), the canonical naming history is a four-stage progression rooted in Kingdom Hearts metaphysics.

Stage I: The "Somebody" Identity (Prakhar)

His legal name is Prakhar Prasun (प्रखर प्रसून). In the fictional naming history behind his nickname, Prakhar serves as his original "Somebody" identity prior to any heart-loss complications.

Stage II: The Organization XIII Nobody (Xaarkph)

In true Kingdom Hearts fashion, things become unnecessarily complicated when he loses his heart and becomes a Nobody. Following standard Organization XIII naming protocol, the Organization takes his legal name (P-R-A-K-H-A-R), adds the traditional X (the Recusant's Sigil), and rearranges the letters into his official Nobody name: Xaarkph.

Stage III: Rebellion and Stealth Gamer Tag (xarkph)

Naturally, he eventually rebels against the Organization and drops the extra "A", creating xarkph as his stealthy gamer tag so he can stay off Xemnas's radar.

Stage IV: The Union χ Lifeboat (Ark)

Eventually, he decided that pronouncing xarkph out loud every time would be ridiculous, so he shortened it to Ark.

This shortened form carries its own second Kingdom Hearts connection: in Kingdom Hearts Union χ, the Ark (also known as the Lifeboat) is a device capable of travelling between worlds and, under certain conditions, through time. Thus, his nickname progresses from his real-world name, through a fictional Nobody identity, into a gamer tag, and finally into a Kingdom Hearts reference about a vessel that carries people between worlds and through time.

"Yes, I know this is an absurd amount of lore for a nickname."

Academic Background and Research

Mechanical Engineering & Postgraduate Goals

After completing his High School Diploma at DAV Public School, Ark enrolled as an undergraduate pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, including an International Exchange at NTNU Trondheim (Norwegian University of Science and Technology). Alongside his engineering training, he is deeply interested in brains and computational neuroscience. After graduating, his longer-term goal is to pursue postgraduate study (a Master's degree) and eventually a PhD in computational neuroscience.

Computational Neuroscience & Connectome Topology

His academic research focuses on computational neuroscience, biological neural networks, and connectome-derived inductive biases in machine learning. Specifically, he has worked on research involving:

  • Biological Connectomes & Network Topology: Investigating the structural organization and graph-theoretic properties of biological neural circuits.
  • Biological vs. Random Network Structures: Comparing real biological connectivity graphs against matched random network baselines to isolate the computational role of biological wiring topology.
  • Motion-Direction Classification & Inductive Biases: Embedding connectome-derived structural constraints into neural network models and evaluating them on spatiotemporal and visual tasks such as motion-direction classification.

Professional and Organizational Experience

Confidential Defence Firm Internship

Ark once interned as a Manufacturing Engineering Intern at a defence firm—an experience that, as he notes, sounds considerably more impressive on paper than it ended up being for his résumé.

Because much of the work was confidential, it strictly limited how much detail he could actually include on his CV afterwards. Nevertheless, the internship gave him firsthand insight into what working on confidential projects is like—and what it feels like to spend months working on things you literally cannot talk about afterwards.

Research, Editorial, and Student Leadership Roles

Across different stages of his university timeline, he has held or undertaken several key academic, editorial, and organizational positions:

  • Undergraduate Research Assistant: Conducted research across computational modelling, computer vision, and biological neural network structures.
  • Manufacturing Engineering Intern: Completed industrial internship work in a confidential defence manufacturing setting.
  • Editor-in-Chief: Led editorial strategy, writing, and publication design for student media.
  • Joint Secretary: Organised technical events, workshops, and digital initiatives in student association leadership.

Personality, Interests, and Habits

Competition, Games, Music, and Fiction Writing

Ark enjoys playing chess and video games, listening to music—including K-pop—and writing, especially fictional stories. He loves competition, whether in games, puzzles, or anything else where there is something to figure out. He particularly enjoys problems where the answer isn't immediately obvious and where experimentation, reasoning, or an unconventional angle is required.

Hyperfixations and the Steel Ball Run Incident

He has a documented tendency to become extremely invested in random things the moment they catch his attention. Most notably, he read the entire JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 7: Steel Ball Run manga in three days—while somehow procrastinating on watching an anime because reading the entire manga at once was apparently the "more efficient option." He also enjoys watching true crime documentaries.

Bizarre Business Ideas and Public Policy

Ark frequently comes up with completely bizarre business ideas and then tries to figure out whether they could actually be profitable. He enjoys reasoning through the underlying incentives:

  • Customer incentives: Why would customers actually pay for it?
  • Employee incentives: Why would employees want to work there, and how do their incentives align?
  • Business models: What mechanism makes the whole operation financially viable?

Sometimes the ideas are completely ridiculous—which is part of the fun. Along the same analytical vein, he also enjoys discussing public policy and analysing whether proposed policy interventions would actually work in practice.

Approach to Life: Novelty and Experience

He strongly enjoys novelty and unfamiliar experiences: trying things he hasn't tried before, visiting unfamiliar places, learning strange facts, or suddenly getting obsessed with a completely random idea. He operates on the belief that there is always another song to hear, another food to taste, another place to visit, another game to play, or another physical thrill yet to be experienced.

Whether it is a bowl of really good butter chicken, discovering a song that instantly becomes a favourite, the excitement of a close game, or even the shock of an ice bath—his approach to life is to keep experiencing things, remain curious, and see what comes next.

Favourites, Taste, and Personal Loadout

Food, Drinks, and the Dishwashing Objection

His favourite food is butter chicken with naan. For drinks, he likes chai and also enjoys alcohol. He genuinely enjoys cooking, although he maintains a strong personal disagreement with the part where he has to wash everything afterwards.

Animals, Husky Shapeshifting, and Charizard

His favourite animals are somewhere between a tiger and a golden retriever. However, if he could freely transform into an animal and back into a human whenever he wanted, he would choose a husky—reasoning that being a fluffy dog with the ability to turn back into a human whenever necessary sounds unbeatable.

His favourite Pokémon is Charizard. Yes, he knows that's a basic choice. He stands by it.

Tech Gifts and Quiet Brands

When it comes to gifts, you can basically never go wrong with technology: receiving a MacBook or an iPhone would make him extremely happy. Aesthetic-wise, he is a bit of a brand guy, specifically preferring quiet, understated brands like The North Face, New Balance, or Apple over loud or showy luxury labels.
